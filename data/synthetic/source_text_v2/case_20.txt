INVITATION TO BID
Owner: Tenmile Basin Recreation District
Project: Tenmile Basin Ice Arena Dehumidification Upgrade
Project Location: Frisco, CO
Trades Required: HVAC and controls
SECTION 1 - SCOPE
Replacement of the arena dehumidification units, new supply ductwork above the seating bowl, and integration of
the new equipment into the District's existing control front end. The ice plant refrigeration system is existing to
remain and is not part of this scope.
SECTION 2 - SCHEDULE AND BUDGET
Engineer's estimate: $890,000
Bid due: November 12, 2026 at 2:00 PM MT
An optional pre-bid walk-through will be held November 3, 2026 at 11:00 AM. Attendance is not mandatory and
bids from firms that do not attend will be accepted.
Construction start: May 24, 2027. Substantial completion: August 20, 2027.
SECTION 3 - BONDS AND INSURANCE
Bid security shall be 10% of the bid amount. Performance and payment bonds are each 100% of the contract
price. Contractor shall carry commercial general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
