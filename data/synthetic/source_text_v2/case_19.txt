REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS - DESIGN BUILD
Owner: Meridian Hollow Hospitality Group
Project: Meridian Hollow Conference Centre Mechanical Design Build
Project Location: Denver, CO
Trades Required: HVAC, piping, sheet metal, and controls
PART A - DELIVERY
This is a design-build procurement. The successful proposer carries design responsibility for the mechanical
systems from schematic through construction documents, and shall carry the allowances listed in Part C within the
proposed price.
PART B - COMMERCIAL
Owner's not-to-exceed budget for this package is $5,500,000.
Proposals due: November 24, 2026 at 3:00 PM MT
Pre-proposal site visit: November 2, 2026 at 10:00 AM
Construction start: July 12, 2027. Substantial completion: April 28, 2028.
PART C - ALLOWANCES
Carry an allowance of $180,000 for owner-directed kitchen exhaust modifications and $95,000 for acoustic
treatment at the rooftop units. Allowances are included within the not-to-exceed figure.
PART D - BONDS AND INSURANCE
Bonding: bid bond 5% of the total bid; performance bond and payment bond each 100% of the contract price.
Commercial general liability $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
