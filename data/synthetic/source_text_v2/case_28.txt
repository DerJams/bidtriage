INVITATION TO BID
Owner: Larkspur Lane Housing Cooperative
Project: Larkspur Lane Boiler Room Repiping
Project Location: Boulder, CO
Trades Required: piping and plumbing

Repiping of the boiler room primary loop, replacement of isolation valves, and new expansion tank. The boilers
themselves are existing to remain.
SECTION 2 - SCHEDULE AND BUDGET
The Cooperative has set a not-to-exceed ceiling of $180,000 for this work. Bids above the ceiling will be rejected.
Bid due: October 26, 2026 at 1:00 PM MT
Pre-bid walk-through: October 12, 2026 at 4:00 PM
Construction start: March 22, 2027. Substantial completion: May 14, 2027.
SECTION 3 - BONDS AND INSURANCE
Bonding: bid bond 5% of the total bid; performance bond and payment bond each 100% of the contract price.
Commercial general liability $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
