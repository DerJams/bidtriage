Good morning,

Cascade Ridge School District 12 is soliciting competitive sealed bids from qualified mechanical contractors.

The project is the Cascade Ridge Middle School RTU Replacement.

The work consists of removal and replacement of fourteen packaged rooftop units serving the classroom wings, associated ductwork modifications, new roof curbs, and full integration into the District's existing building automation system.

Trades required: HVAC, sheet metal, and controls.

Site: Cascade Ridge Middle School, Arvada, CO.

Mandatory pre-bid walk-through: September 11, 2026 at 9:00 AM local time. Attendance is mandatory.

Sealed bids are due no later than September 25, 2026 at 2:00 PM MT.

The District's engineer has established a construction budget of $850,000.

Bid security shall be 10% of the bid amount. Performance and payment bonds are each 100% of the contract price. Contractor shall carry commercial general liability of $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.

Construction is scheduled to commence February 15, 2027 with substantial completion required by July 30, 2027.
