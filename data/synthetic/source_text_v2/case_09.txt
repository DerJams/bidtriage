INVITATION TO BID
Owner: Vantage Point Logistics
Project: Distribution Center Ventilation Upgrade
Project Location: Longmont, CO
Trades Required: sheet metal and HVAC
SECTION 1 - SCOPE OF WORK
Fabrication and installation of a warehouse ventilation and destratification system across 640,000 square feet:
spiral supply ductwork, gravity relief ventilators, dock make-up air units, and unit heaters. Controls integration is
by the Owner's existing vendor and is excluded.

Engineer's estimate: $2,200,000
Bid due: November 10, 2026 at 4:00 PM MT
Pre-bid walk-through: October 22, 2026 at 9:00 AM
Construction start: May 3, 2027. Substantial completion: October 15, 2027.
SECTION 3 - BONDING AND INSURANCE
Bonding: bid bond 5% of the total bid; performance bond and payment bond each 100% of the contract price.
Commercial general liability $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
