INVITATION TO BID - PHASED CONSTRUCTION
Owner: Bramblewood Senior Living Trust
Project: Bramblewood Campus Mechanical Renewal
Project Location: Boulder, CO
Trades Required: HVAC, plumbing, and controls
SECTION 1 - PHASING
The Work is phased to keep the campus occupied throughout. Phase 1 covers the north residence wing. Phase 2
covers the commons and clinic. Both phases are awarded under a single contract and a single bid.
Phase 1 construction: October 26, 2026 through February 12, 2027. Phase 2 construction: March 1, 2027 through
August 6, 2027. The contract construction window therefore runs from the Phase 1 start to the Phase 2
completion.
SECTION 2 - SCHEDULE AND BUDGET
Engineer's estimate: $1,980,000
Bid due: October 8, 2026 at 2:00 PM MT
Pre-bid walk-through: September 23, 2026 at 9:00 AM
SECTION 3 - BONDS AND INSURANCE
Bonding: bid bond 5% of the total bid; performance bond and payment bond each 100% of the contract price.
Commercial general liability $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
