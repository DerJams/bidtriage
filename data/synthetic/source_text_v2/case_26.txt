REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS AND BUDGETARY INPUT
Owner: Chapel Rock Congregational Trust
Project: Chapel Rock Sanctuary Mechanical Renewal
Project Location: Golden, CO
Trades Required: HVAC and piping
PART 1 - PROCUREMENT METHOD
This is a negotiated procurement. The Trust is not issuing a competitive bid and there is no sealed bid deadline.
Interested contractors are asked to submit qualifications and budgetary input, after which the Trust will negotiate a
contract with a single firm.
PART 2 - SCOPE
Replacement of the sanctuary air handling equipment and the associated hydronic piping, with attention to
acoustic performance during services.
PART 3 - COMMERCIAL
No construction budget has been established. Establishing a budget is part of the requested budgetary input.
Construction dates will be set during negotiation.
Site tour: October 14, 2026 at 10:00 AM
Bonding: bid bond 5% of the total bid; performance bond and payment bond each 100% of the contract price.
Commercial general liability $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
