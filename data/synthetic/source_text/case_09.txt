INVITATION TO BID
VANTAGE POINT LOGISTICS

Project Distribution Center Ventilation Upgrade
Owner Vantage Point Logistics
Project Location Longmont, CO
Trades Required sheet metal and HVAC
SECTION 1 - SCOPE OF WORK
Fabrication and installation of a new warehouse ventilation and destratification system across 640,000 square feet
of distribution space, including spiral supply ductwork, gravity relief ventilators, make-up air units serving the dock
doors, and unit heaters throughout. Controls integration is by the Owner existing vendor and is excluded from this
package.
