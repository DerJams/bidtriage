INVITATION TO BID
PIKES HOLLOW ATHLETIC CLUB
Solicitation No. PHAC-2026-POOL-01
Project Natatorium Pool Mechanical Replacement
Owner Pikes Hollow Athletic Club
Project Location Colorado Springs, CO
Trades Required plumbing and process piping
SECTION 1 - SCOPE OF WORK
Complete replacement of the natatorium pool mechanical systems, including circulation pumps, regenerative
media filters, chemical feed and controller equipment, surge tank piping, and all associated CPVC and copper
distribution piping. Pool dehumidification equipment is existing to remain and is not part of this scope.
SECTION 2 - SCHEDULE OF QUANTITIES

22 11 16 Type L copper piping, 2 in. and smaller LF 460
Quantities shown are for bidder convenience. Bidders are responsible for verifying all quantities from the contract drawings.
SECTION 3 - PROJECT SCHEDULE AND BUDGET
Engineer's estimate $600,000
Bid due October 14, 2026 at 5:00 PM MT
Construction start February 8, 2027
Substantial completion May 29, 2027
A pre-bid walk-through will not be held for this project. Bidders may arrange individual site access by appointment
with the Club.
SECTION 4 - BONDING AND INSURANCE
This is a privately funded project. No bid bond, performance bond, or payment bond is required for this solicitation.
Bidders shall provide a current certificate of insurance upon award.
