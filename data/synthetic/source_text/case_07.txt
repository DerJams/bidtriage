INVITATION TO BID
CACHE LA POUDRE COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT
Solicitation No. CLPCC-2026-ITB-041
Project Science Building HVAC Modernization
Owner Cache La Poudre Community College District
Project Location Fort Collins, CO
Trades Required HVAC, sheet metal, and controls
SECTION 1 - SCOPE OF WORK
Replacement of the Science Building original constant-volume air handling equipment with variable-air-volume
systems, including new galvanized supply and return ductwork throughout three floors, fume hood exhaust
manifolding, energy recovery ventilator installation, and a full building automation system replacement. Laboratory
process piping is by others and is excluded from this package.
